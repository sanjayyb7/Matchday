# Matchday — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Football fans in San Francisco heading to pubs for live matches. They use the app on mobile, often while walking, in a social/out-with-friends context. Job: find where fans are gathering, pick a player identity, chat with their squad, and feel part of the matchday crowd.

### Brand Personality
**Playful · Social · Spontaneous**

Emotional goals: fun, belonging, in-the-moment energy — like snapping friends at the pub, not reading a sports dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Snapchat-inspired (partial)** — camera-first circular avatars, rounded chat bubbles, stories-style live fan row. Not a Snap clone; football meetup context stays primary.

**Theme: Hybrid**
- Light chat screen (white/soft gray, Snap yellow accents)
- Dark map & profile (stadium night energy)

**References:** Snapchat chat UI, stories row, yellow accent, playful rounded shapes  
**Anti-references:** Corporate dashboard, FIFA game menu density, generic dark SaaS

### Design Principles
1. **Circles first** — avatars, story rings, and bubbles over sharp rectangles
2. **Light where you talk** — chat feels airy and social; map stays immersive and dark
3. **Yellow = you** — Snap-style yellow for own messages and primary CTAs
4. **Stories = live squad** — who's here now, not a static roster
5. **Minimal chrome** — nav and headers stay out of the way; content is the hero
